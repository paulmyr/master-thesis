\section{Loss Landscape Analysis}\label{sec:app:loss}
\ierror{This whole section reads like Methodology + Results, not appendix material. The setup paragraph (28 pairs, 11{,}200 simulations, 3.8\,h compute) belongs in Methodology, and the cross-loss plateau comparison (todo at end of section) belongs in Results. Consider moving the prose into \texttt{4\_methodology.tex} / \texttt{5\_results.tex} and leaving only figure plates here, if anything.}
To characterise the optimisation problem independently of any particular training run, we computed each of the four loss functions on a dense two-parameter grid ($20 \times 20 = 400$ points per pair) for 28 parameter pairs, for a total of $11{,}200$ simulations and $\sim$3.8\,h of compute.
The remaining parameters were held at the default initial values of \cref{tab:adex_params}.
